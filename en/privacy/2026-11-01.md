# Privacy Policy

Nostra (the "App") values users' personal information and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other relevant laws and regulations.

This policy explains the scope of information processed when using the App and how it is processed. The App has no sign-up, and the Operator does not run any server of its own. However, the external services below process some information in order to display ads and process payments for paid products.

## 1. Personal Information Processed and Purposes

The Operator does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses or payment method information. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

### ① Data stored only on the device (required)

- Numbers generated in the App, numbers registered by manual entry or QR code, the results of checking them, and the [I bought this] mark
- Spending, prizes and notes entered manually in the ledger, prize-claimed marks, the monthly budget, and the history of winning store alerts
- Display mode, language, notification and vibration settings, the generation method last selected, the number of generations per day, and a cache of purchase status
- A copy of the winning numbers for recent draws — downloaded in advance so they can be viewed offline
- This data exists only in this device's storage and is not transmitted to the Operator. A file the user creates with [Settings > Data > Export records] is saved or sent only where the user chooses, and does not contain location.

### ② Payment and purchase verification (when opening the purchase screen, purchasing or restoring a paid product, or launching the App on a device that has made a purchase)

- Items: an anonymous user ID specific to the App, store purchase receipt, transaction identifier, product purchased, purchase and expiration times, device type and OS version, IP address, and language and country (currency) settings
- Purpose: purchase verification, checking subscription status, and restoring purchases
- Legal basis: conclusion and performance of a contract (Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) Article 15(1)4)

### ③ Advertising (users who have not turned off ads)

- Items: advertising identifier (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA if tracking is allowed), IP address, device and OS information, and ad impression and click records
- Purpose: ad serving, frequency capping, performance measurement and fraudulent click prevention, and personalized ads if you consent
- Google AdMob collects this directly. For details, see Section 5 (Behavioral Information).

### ④ Temporary data requiring device permissions (optional)

- Camera — used only at the moment a QR code is read; no camera footage is stored or transmitted.
- Notifications — only notifications generated by the device itself (draws, sales deadlines, winning stores, prize claim deadlines) are used. No push token is stored on a server, and the neighborhood or location you choose is used on the device only to decide which alerts to show.
- Files — only the file the user picks in [Import records] is read; no other files are accessed.

### ⑤ Approximate location (optional — only when turning on the radius criterion for winning store alerts)

- The App reads the device's approximate location only once, when the user taps [Winning store alerts > Use current location]. It does not track location continuously and does not read location in the background.
- The location read is reduced to a precision of about 1 km, stored only on this device, and used only to calculate, on the device, the distance to newly published winning stores.
- The location is not transmitted to the Operator or any third party. It is erased immediately when you tap [Clear location] or delete the App.
- Instead of using location, you can receive the same alerts by directly choosing a province/metropolitan city and a city/county/district.

### ⑥ App update checks (required)

- Items: IP address, device type and OS, runtime version and update channel, a random installation identifier the update server uses to tell requests apart (a value generated and stored on the device the first time the App runs), and, if the App stopped with an error on the previous launch, that error message (up to 1,024 characters)
- Purpose: checking for and downloading app fixes (updates)
- Processed by Expo's (650 Industries, Inc.) update server when the App is launched and when you return to it. The Operator does not use this information to identify users.

### ⑦ Downloading public data (required)

- Items: IP address, device and app information (User-Agent), and the path of the requested file
- Purpose: providing public data such as winning numbers, winning stores, policies and notices
- The data is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and request records may remain in GitHub's server logs. The Operator does not receive these logs.

## 2. Retention Period and Destruction

- Data on the device is deleted together with the App when the App is deleted. The Operator does not hold a copy of it. However, if the device's OS backup (iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, the App's data may be included in that backup, and its retention follows Apple's or Google's policies.
- The criteria for winning store alerts (the chosen neighborhood and the reduced location) remain only on this device until the user clears them or deletes the App.
- An exported records file remains wherever the user chose to keep it until the user deletes it.
- Purchase information held by RevenueCat is retained until the purchase or subscription relationship ends or deletion is requested. However, pursuant to Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, records of contracts, withdrawal of subscriptions and payment may be retained for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution for 3 years.
- Retention of advertising-related information follows Google's policies.
- Information whose retention period has ended is to be destroyed without delay using a method that makes it unrecoverable.

## 3. Outsourcing of Processing

The Operator outsources work as follows for payment verification, app update delivery and publishing public data.

- RevenueCat, Inc. — in-app purchase receipt verification and subscription status management (sub-outsourcee: Amazon Web Services, Inc. — data storage)
- 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo) — app update delivery
- GitHub, Inc. — publishing public data (draws, winning stores, policies, notices)

## 4. Overseas Collection and Transfer of Personal Information

The information below is transferred to businesses located in the United States (Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) Article 28-8).

### ① RevenueCat, Inc.

- Country: United States · Contact: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

- Purpose: purchase verification and subscription management · Retention period: as set out in Section 2
- Timing and method: transmitted over the network (HTTPS) when opening the purchase screen, when purchasing or restoring, and when the App is launched on a device that has made a purchase
- Legal basis: outsourcing and storage of processing for the performance of a contract (Article 28-8(1)3)

### ② Google LLC (AdMob)

- Country: United States · Contact: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Items: the items in 1-③
- Purpose: ad serving, measurement and fraudulent click prevention, and personalized ads if you consent · Retention period: in accordance with Google's policies
- Timing and method: transmitted over the network (HTTPS) when ads are requested and displayed

### ③ 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo)

- Country: United States · Contact: https://expo.dev/privacy
- Items: the items in 1-⑥
- Purpose: checking for and delivering app updates · Retention period: in accordance with Expo's policies
- Timing and method: transmitted over the network (HTTPS) when the App is launched or you return to it

### ④ GitHub, Inc.

- Country: United States · Contact: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- Items: the items in 1-⑦
- Purpose: publishing public data · Retention period: in accordance with GitHub's policies
- Timing and method: transmitted over the network (HTTPS) when the App downloads public data

### ⑤ How to refuse and its effects

- If you do not open the purchase screen and do not purchase or restore a paid product, no information is transmitted to RevenueCat. However, you cannot use paid features.
- The advertising transfer stops if you purchase the product that turns off ads. You can refuse personalized ads using the methods in Section 5; even if you refuse, non-personalized ads are still displayed.
- App update checks and downloading public data are needed for the App to work and cannot be turned off separately. If you do not want them, please delete the App.

## 5. Collection of Behavioral Information and How to Refuse

- The Google AdMob SDK is installed in the App, so Google directly collects the advertising identifier and ad impression and click information within the App. The Operator does not directly collect or store this information and does not collect sensitive behavioral information.
- iOS — you can refuse the tracking permission request on first launch, or turn it off in [Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking].
- Android — you can delete the advertising ID in [Settings > Google > Ads].
- EEA, UK and Switzerland — you can choose your consent again in the App's [Settings > Ad privacy settings].
- If you refuse, non-personalized ads are displayed, and you receive rewards from rewarded ads all the same.
- How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 6. Children's Personal Information

The App is intended for people aged 19 or older (full age) and does not knowingly collect personal information of children under the age of 14.

## 7. Users' Rights and How to Exercise Them

- Numbers and records on the device are all erased immediately via [Settings > Data > Delete everything] or when the App is deleted.
- You can get your records as a file via [Settings > Data > Export records] and move them to another device.
- You can request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of purchase information by emailing the address below. The Operator has no information that identifies users, so please include your store order number (receipt) with your request.
- Notification permission can be turned off at any time in the App's settings or the OS settings.
- Location permission can be revoked at any time in the OS settings, and a saved location can be erased right away via [Winning store alerts > Clear location].
- Consent to personalized ads can be withdrawn using the methods in Section 5.

## 8. Security Measures

- All communications made by the App are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Number generation and record storage take place only on the device, so there are no records that could leak in transit in the first place.
- Exported records files are not encrypted, so keep them only in places you trust.
- Payment method information is not handled by the Operator and is processed by the stores. Outsourcees are managed through data processing agreements (DPAs).
- We do not collect any information beyond what is necessary. The App has no login or administrator features, so there are no accounts to be stolen.

- Transfers to the United States rely on lawful transfer mechanisms, such as standard contractual clauses adopted by the outsourcees and Google.
