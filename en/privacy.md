Nostra (the "App") values users' personal information and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other relevant laws and regulations.

This policy explains the scope of information collected when using the App and how it is handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

There is no sign-up, and we do not collect general personal information such as names, phone numbers or email addresses. The App also does not create or keep any device identifier.

### ① Data stored only on the device (required)

- Numbers generated in the App, numbers registered by manual entry or QR code, and the results of checking them
- Display mode, notification settings, the generation method last selected, and the number of generations per day
- A copy of the winning numbers for recent draws — downloaded in advance so they can be viewed offline
- This data exists only in this device's storage and is not transmitted anywhere.

### ② Advertising identifier (optional)

- When you view an ad, Google AdMob collects the advertising identifier and device information.
- If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are requested, and you receive ad rewards all the same.

### ③ Temporary data requiring device permissions (optional)

- Camera — used only at the moment a QR code is read; no camera footage is stored or transmitted.
- Notifications — only notifications generated by the device itself are used. No push token is stored on a server.

## 2. Purposes of Collection and Use

- Providing number generation, record-keeping and result-checking features
- Displaying statistical test results of each method's performance
- Draw result and sales closing notifications (if you consent)
- Serving ads and preventing fraudulent clicks (AdMob)

## 3. Retention Period

Data stored by the App is kept only on this device and is deleted together with the App when the App is deleted. The developer does not hold a copy of this data, so there is no such thing as a server retention period.

Retention of advertising-related data follows Google's policies.

## 4. Provision to Third Parties and Outsourcing

Except for the following, we do not provide any data to outside parties.

- Google AdMob — ad delivery and performance measurement based on the advertising identifier. Numbers and records saved by users are not transmitted.
- Public static file host — the App only downloads draw data from it. No personal information is sent in doing so. The App does not connect directly to the lottery operator.

## 5. Users' Rights

- Saved numbers and records exist only on the device, so deleting the App immediately erases all of them.
- Notification permission can be turned off at any time in the App's settings or the OS settings.
- Consent to personalized ads can be chosen again in Settings → Ad privacy settings. It can also be turned off by deleting the advertising ID in the Android device settings.
- The developer holds no personal information, so there is nothing for which access, correction or deletion can be requested.

## 6. Security Measures

- All communications made by the App are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Number generation and record storage take place only on the device, so there is no data that could leak in transit in the first place.
- The App has no login, payment or administrator features, so there are no accounts or credentials to be stolen.

## 7. Notice of Changes

If this policy changes, we will update both the text on this screen and the effective date at the top, and announce it through an App update. Significant changes will be announced 7 days before the effective date.
